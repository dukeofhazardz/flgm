**2. ABOUT US PAGE**  
**About Forever Life and Grace Mission**  
**Who We Are**  
Forever Life and Grace Mission (FLGM) is a Christ-centred Christian ministry committed to the revelation of God’s grace, eternal life, sonship, and the spiritual advancement of believers.  
We are a community of believers who recognise that the Christian life is founded on the finished work of Jesus Christ and the reality of God’s life in those who believe.  
Our ministry emphasises spiritual understanding, personal transformation, fellowship, discipleship, evangelism, and Kingdom influence.  
We believe that every believer has a divine purpose and is called to manifest the life, wisdom, and character of God in every sphere of life.  
At Forever Life and Grace Mission, our commitment is to help believers understand who they are in Christ, grow in grace, and live out the fullness of their divine identity.  
**Our History**  
Forever Life and Grace Mission was established as a ministry dedicated to the revelation of God’s grace and eternal life in Jesus Christ.  
Over the years, the ministry has continued to pursue its mandate of raising believers in spiritual understanding, sonship, and Kingdom influence through teaching, worship, prayer, fellowship, and evangelism.  
Through its various ministry expressions, FLGM seeks to strengthen believers, nurture young people, build godly relationships, and extend the message of God’s grace to communities and beyond.  
Our journey continues with a commitment to spiritual growth, discipleship, evangelism, and the establishment of a strong church community.  
Editorial note: The church’s founding year, founder’s name, major historical milestones, and significant ministry achievements should be added once verified by the church leadership.  
**Our Vision**  
To raise a people established in the revelation of God’s grace and eternal life, manifesting sonship, dominion, spiritual maturity, and Kingdom influence in every sphere of life.  
We envision a generation of believers who:  
* Understand and live from their identity as sons of God.  
* Are established in the knowledge of God’s grace and eternal life.  
* Demonstrate spiritual maturity and the character of Christ.  
* Exercise dominion and influence in their families, communities, and society.  
* Participate actively in evangelism, discipleship, and the advancement of God’s Kingdom.  
**Our Mission**  
Our mission is to:  
* Reveal God’s grace and eternal life: Teach the finished work of Jesus Christ and the life believers have received in Him.  
* Establish believers in sonship: Help Christians understand their identity, inheritance, and position in Christ.  
* Promote spiritual growth: Encourage believers to grow in grace, faith, knowledge, and spiritual maturity.  
* Raise Kingdom influencers: Equip believers to manifest God’s wisdom, love, and dominion in their various spheres of influence.  
* Advance evangelism and discipleship: Reach people with the Gospel and nurture them into spiritually mature believers.  
* Build a strong community of faith: Foster meaningful fellowship, unity, service, and mutual edification.  
**Our Beliefs**  
Forever Life and Grace Mission is founded on the following biblical convictions:  
1. The Lordship of Jesus Christ  
We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God, our Lord and Saviour, through whom we receive salvation, righteousness, and eternal life.  
2. The Grace of God  
We believe salvation and our standing before God are established through His grace and the finished work of Jesus Christ, not by human effort or self-righteousness.  
3. Eternal Life  
We believe eternal life is the life of God received through Jesus Christ and that believers are called to live in the reality and expression of this life.  
4. Sonship and the New Creation  
We believe those who are in Christ are new creations and have received the privilege and identity of sons of God.  
5. The Authority of Scripture  
We believe the Holy Scriptures are foundational to our faith, doctrine, spiritual instruction, and Christian living.  
6. The Holy Spirit  
We believe in the person and work of the Holy Spirit, who empowers, guides, teaches, and enables believers to live and serve according to God’s purpose.  
7. The Church and Kingdom Influence  
We believe the Church is called to proclaim the Gospel, nurture believers, demonstrate God’s love, and influence the world with the values of His Kingdom.  
These beliefs are a proposed summary based on the ministry’s stated themes. The church’s official doctrinal statement should be reviewed and approved by the Chief Pastor before publication.
